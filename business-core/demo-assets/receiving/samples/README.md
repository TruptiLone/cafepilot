# Sample photos for the public demo

Visitors can tap one of these on **Receive delivery** instead of uploading their own photo. Each one goes to Muse
exactly like an upload.

These are real photos from the recorded demo (Nov 4 2026), cropped to just the slip or the counter. They follow the
market story:

| File | What it shows |
|---|---|
| `slip-PO-123-willow-ridge.jpg` | Willow Ridge Dairy Supply slip for PO-123: 4 half gallons whole milk, 1 bag coffee beans, 10 croissants |
| `delivery-PO-123-short.jpg` | What arrived for PO-123: 3 half gallons (one short), the beans, croissants in a bowl (Muse asks the owner to count them) |
| `slip-PO-124-bay-dairy.jpg` | Bay Dairy slip for the reorder PO-124: 1 half gallon whole milk |
| `delivery-PO-124.jpg` | What arrived for PO-124: 1 half gallon |

PO-123 is the first order a fresh demo makes, so the numbers match each visitor's own order. Fictional suppliers and
synthetic order data. The list the app offers is in `backend/modules/receiving/samples.py`.
